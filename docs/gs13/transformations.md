# Transformation implementation record

Source: `Gain-Station-13/GS13-Blubberstation@ec3ad13bd34b64451813004e32892d49de745404`.

This record covers the first two items in the ordered completion queue. It does not close the broader appearance, anatomy, or release milestones.

## Shared body state

Source blueberry juice and Starshine Neutron contribute `124 * volume² / 1000` to apparent BFI through the source hider interface. The native `transformations.dm` adapter aggregates independent, replaceable sources, derives the existing nine body stages, and removes a source when its datum is deleted. The contribution does not rewrite regular fat, permanent fat, DNA, or starting body size. Existing concealment, maximum apparent weight, temporary resizing, and native anatomy still apply.

A dedicated native color priority sits below temporary/admin effects and above washable paint. Clearing the transformation reveals the current underlying colors; it does not restore an old snapshot over paint or a species change made during the effect. The last registered colored transformation has priority within this slot. Removing it reveals the preceding active transformation.

Reagent additions, merges, drainage, transfers, metabolism, live preference edits, and native species changes refresh the state. Effects stay with the physical body across mind transfers and disconnects. A corpse keeps the mass of its retained chemicals and still shrinks when drained. Reagent or body deletion removes the owned state and listeners. Ordinary character/DNA snapshots do not copy active chemical effects to another body.

## Blueberry juice foundation

The independent `blueberry_inflation` setting defaults to false, matching source. It uses the existing character JSON/SQL preference field. An opted-out body immediately discards juice; a live opt-out also removes an existing dose. This setting does not enable ordinary chemical weight gain. Eligible organic carbons use the source color and volume formula. Synthetic species that cannot metabolize the reagent do not receive its body effect. The inherited `no_mob_color` option retains volume growth without recoloring.

The current human appearance adapter displays the resulting stages even when ordinary weight gain is disabled. Other species retain their native silhouette until item 3 supplies their body-specific adapters. The complete source's separate organ/body-shape customization is still pending.

## Disease, production, crops, and treatment

The Berrification symptom is available to native virology and as a direct disease preset. With blueberry transformations enabled it seeds `max(1, stage speed) * 10` juice, then adds `max(stage speed, 0.2) * stage` every twenty seconds. The ordinary chemical/viral weight-gain switches remain separate from this transformation opt-in, matching the source's specific blueberry gate. Native disease treatments/cures remain applicable. Removing all juice cures the infection without granting resistance; an ordinary disease cure stops production and sound while leaving existing juice to drain or metabolize. The source's native symptom API and timing are adapted rather than duplicating its parent-Start call.

Multiple infections share one original sound loop per body. Death mutes it; revival of an active infection restarts it. Disease, source, or body deletion stops and deletes the loop. The symptom releases its body listeners on removal.

Normal berries can mutate into blueberries while retaining their existing mutation choices. The source's low starting potency, yield, production timing, reagent coefficient, and original sprites are retained. Native planting, harvesting, seed extraction, grinding, and juicing supply ordinary acquisition and processing. Grinding extracts transformation juice; the source's juicing product remains ordinary berry juice. Wine strength uses Paradise's 0-to-1 scale.

To collect juice, use an open glass container on a blueberry in **grab intent**. This maps the source secondary-click action to Paradise's native intent system. Every one-second dose rechecks the held container, its lid and capacity, adjacency, and the remaining juice. It transfers only blueberry juice, at most ten units and the configured transfer amount per dose, preserving unrelated medicines. Fully draining an infected body also cures Berrification. Native viral contamination is retained on juice collected from an infected host.

Permaberry is a native neutral quirk and can also be acquired from a juice dose of at least 999 units during metabolism. It produces `(0.01 + min(0.49, elapsed seconds / 14693))` units per second, including after death as in source, subject to blueberry opt-in and species metabolism. Paradise processing supplies deciseconds, which are converted once. Fractional production accumulates until a new dose survives the native 0.09-unit removal boundary. Removing the quirk stops its actual processing subsystem.

## Capacity and bursting

The character editor now includes the source blueberry capacity, survival, safe-count, automatic acceptance, viewing, and remains settings, plus the glutton fullness/fatness capacities, six outcomes, viewing, remains, and feedback toggles. Capacity defaults to zero and the glutton outcome defaults to Disabled. Blueberry survival defaults to true; automatic acceptance and viewing default to false. These keys use the existing per-character JSON/SQL snapshots. Invalid values remain disabled; safe-count allowances use whole numbers.

Blueberry capacity follows the source's 40/70/90 percent feedback intervals and requires strictly more than the configured volume to trigger. Apparent weight caps/concealment do not change actual fluid pressure. Source cosmetic streaks retain the dose; puddle leaks transfer two percent, up to 100 units. Paradise uses native cleanable, drainable reagent decals in place of the source liquid-turf subsystem. Repeated leaks reuse one decal of each type per turf. Spilled volume is conserved, and infected spill data uses native viral contamination. Burst splash follows native line of sight and breathing protection, with range bounded to seven tiles.

Fullness pressure is measured above 750, divided by its configured allowance. Fatness feedback starts at 60 percent of the selected BFI threshold and reaches capacity at the threshold. The larger pressure wins. The original disabled-capacity sentinel is retained on the human body. A safe glutton burst clears reagents through native deletion callbacks, resets fullness, and reduces regular then permanent fat to `max(0, threshold * 0.6 - 150)` where needed. Injure/Critical apply source chest and over-capacity limb trauma. The later native anatomy adapter adds 30/100 stomach trauma when fullness pressure is strictly greater than one, using a separate slot that preserves Paradise nutriment implants. Native chest trauma retains its normal random internal-organ injury behavior. See [the anatomy record](anatomy.md) for treatment and lifecycle coverage. Fatal removes the body from the round, optionally leaving remains; Gib uses ordinary native gibbing.

Without automatic blueberry acceptance, a native dialog offers Delay or Burst now. Dismissal delays. Fatal blueberry and all glutton choices require the second source confirmation. Blueberry preparation lasts seven seconds; glutton preparation lasts five seconds followed by six seconds of swelling. Movement interrupts preparation. Recovery, changed outcome/settings, death, disconnect, or a newly active stabilizing field cancels pending effects. Every delayed callback revalidates the generation, current outcome, capacity, and nearby viewers. A previous safe confirmation cannot execute after a switch to a fatal setting. Offline bodies keep their state but do not initiate dialogs or automatic bursts.

Safe blueberry bursts use a body-owned counter. A finite allowance protects only while the counter is strictly below the selected number; exhausted/negative remaining counts cannot grant extra lives. Fatal removal preserves equipment and contained mobs. Swelling composes with native size/lying changes and removes only its own multipliers and stun. It does not restore an old transform over later body changes. Viewing checks use actual player viewpoints and saved preferences, including remote eyes and rectangular viewports; direct client verification remains required.

The WT field collar retains native neck-slot locking/key storage and original source object, worn, hand, and light layers. A source-owned no-burst trait protects only its actual wearer. Dropping, moving, deleting, or disabling it clears that source while preserving other immunity. It uses an integrated native signaler at electropack frequency/code 2. The existing Electropack panel supplies power/frequency/code/reset controls; power here toggles the stabilizing field. Control actions recheck the holder, hands, numeric bounds, and current location. Matching ordinary native remote signals toggle the field. Its protolathe design requires Materials 4, Biotech 4, Bluespace 3 and the source material proportions.

## Starshine and cyborg palette

Nova, Yellow Dwarf, Orange Giant, and Neutron use native reagent IDs, ordinary nutrition, source-colored volume-driven lights, and the source three-ingredient recipes. The Neutron recipe uses native `bluespace_dust`. Ordinary and handheld soda fountains supply Nova; the latter is the native equivalent of the source soda synthesizer. Each drink owns its own light object, updates it on dose changes, stops it on death, and rebuilds it on revival. Removing the reagent/holder/body releases its light and listeners without changing another light source. Range/power retain the source formula up to the normal body's 1000-unit dose and are bounded at that dose's output for larger admin volumes.

Neutron contributes the same squared-volume BFI as blueberry juice and retains source organic/synthetic processing. Its temporary mass now honors the master and chemical weight preferences; the original unconditional hider would bypass them. Its ordinary glow/nutrition remain separate. It composes with independent blueberry mass/color, follows transfers and species changes, and does not rewrite underlying fat. Native minor addiction represents the source's craving messages. The later [fluid-organ adapter](fluid_organs.md) implements the actual overdose: it changes the existing optional organs' product and enables breast lactation, preserving stored fluid and birth DNA. Those changes persist through removal of the drink, repair/revival, and physical transplantation.

Source artwork is preserved for all four filled/empty containers, generic native glass styles, the vendor, and the refill. The native paid vendor offers thirty of each ordinary flavor and ten Neutron. Neutron is a higher-priced ordinary product because Paradise does not have the source premium product category. Prices are 60/120 native credits. The standard vendor-board selector can construct it; its refill is also available in a 100-credit native vending supply crate, twice the ordinary native refill-crate baseline. Vendor overlay lookup supports both native underscore and source hyphen suffixes while retaining native state precedence.

The expanded cyborg palette now contains blueberry juice and Neutron as its final two choices, for nine expanded or twelve hacked reservoirs.
