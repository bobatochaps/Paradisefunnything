# GS13 to Paradise port

**Work in progress. This is not a complete GS13 port.**

Source: `sheepishgoat/GS13-Blubberstation@ec3ad13bd34b64451813004e32892d49de745404`.
Original base: `ParadiseSS13/Paradise@7084a06407db70b41389045dd38f54ae3eb2d1f1`.
Current integration target: `ParadiseSS13/Paradise@4657875a140bf9b49416a154fe4bc4cd364ce925` (master inspected 2026-10-17; merged and validated).

The [completion plan](gs13/completion_plan.md) and [source inventory](gs13/source_inventory.md) now track the complete modular file set, historical source delta, inherited dependency candidates, and completion gates. Their discovery counts are not a completion percentage.

## Implemented foundation

- Per-character preferences: master switch, source switches, starting weight, gain/loss multipliers, cap, permanent gain, and muscle gain. Gain is disabled by default, as in GS13.
- Native Paradise character SQL save/load integration and character-setup editor.
- Regular/permanent/apparent weight, rate modifiers, muscle, exercise, excess-nutrition conversion, movement penalty, and GS13 HUD alerts.
- Weight analyzer, floor scale, bluespace concealment belt, autolathe analyzer design, and cargo weighing-equipment crate.
- Initial chemistry: lipoifier, weak lipoifier, galbanic compound, macerinic solution, lipolicide integration, and caramel as a recipe ingredient. Overdoses preserve their normal GS13 effects.
- Strong/weak legs and lipolicide/macerinic tolerance quirks through Paradise character setup. Quirk costs use Paradise's smaller point scale.
- Fullness from nutrition and undigested food, temporary bloating/hunger recovery, larger feeding limits, fullness HUD and examine descriptions, and burp relief with a cooldown.
- Fizulphite/extilphite chemistry and galbanic addiction adapted to Paradise metabolism and withdrawal stages.
- Weight Gain disease symptom, available through native virology, with GS13 source preferences and lipolicide treatment.
- Fatoray variants, Salamander and Matador energy guns, and AL-T-Ray metabolism/reversal modes using Paradise's firing, armor, and cell systems. Original gun/projectile/in-hand sprites are included.
- Calorite ore generation, mining scan sprites, sheets, smelting/ORM points, R&D and recycler storage, and Basic Fatoray/cannon and AL-T-Ray research designs. Mining rarity follows gold in Paradise's ore tables; research uses native technology levels in place of TG techweb nodes.
- Calorite contact component, micro-calorite chemistry, severe exposure effects, and permanent Fatoray conversion. Exposure requires the new severe-effects preference; chemical and permanent-weight preferences remain independent. GS13's ordinary/permanent weight damage types are integrated with native armor and fall back to stamina when weight weapons are disabled.
- Calorite/galbanic whips and hammers, fattening batons and stunsword, native cargo and uplink distribution for ported weapons, and a researchable Fatoray cyborg upgrade. Stunsword stunning uses Paradise baton mechanics; its weight damage, size, sprites, and upgraded cell follow GS13. Cargo prices preserve ratios to native energy gun prices.
- Calorite walls/falsewalls, four floor variants, doors, and statues with source sprites. Native sheet recipes, girder construction, tile installation/recovery, material recovery, and air sealing are wired in. Door trapping uses an independent native timed status effect that respects item preferences.
- Lipoifier, galbanic, and mixed chemical smoke grenades; six prepared reagent beakers; ordinary/syndicate calorite pens; and the feeder mini energy crossbow. The Adipo-Warfare uplink now contains eighteen ported entries, including the pendant and hacked cyborg tube. The crossbow retains Paradise incapacitation, scales its payload with native armor, and respects weapon and chemical preferences independently.
- Memento Calori pendant with source ground/worn sprites, a neck-slot action, one binding per body, concealment of both weight channels, and the source removal penalties. Native movement/deletion cleanup prevents stale concealment and duplicate penalties.
- Cyborg feeding-tube base, expansion, and hacked modules with native installation, reagent selection, injection protection, and powered synthesis. Each reagent reservoir holds 25 units and refills five units every twelve seconds at 75 cell charge per depleted reservoir. Base/expanded designs are available in the exosuit fabricator; the hacked module is in the uplink. Alien honey and strawberry milk (including its mixing recipe) support the expanded palette.
- Freestanding feeding machine and portable blender, including a native control panel, adjustable flow, recipient disconnect alert, cargo crate, and safe container/connection cleanup. Food that cannot fit in the beaker remains available for another blend. Native mouth access and ingestion replace TG stomach-organ dependencies.
- Adipoelectric generator and transformer with native regional-powernet connections, circuit-imprinter research, machine-frame construction, stock-part upgrades, occupant handling, source EMP behavior, and original platform sprites. Power output is reported in watts independent of processing interval; transformer loading includes the complete consumed surplus.
- Caloray continuous energy-transfer gun, both conversion modes, removable native cells, source variants and sprites, protolathe research, and native range/obstruction checks. Caloray crossings do not trigger medical-beam explosions. Recharge uses actual regular weight removed and is limited by free cell capacity.
- First native appearance adapter: nine human weight stages, original belly/hip sprites, modular grey and departmental uniform variants, fullness panels, skin-tone updates, dyeing/rolling, clothing concealment, and limb-safe cleanup. Other species and additional clothing families still need adapters.
- Calorite and bluespace collars: original sprites, native neck slots, key locks and treat storage, dynamic gain modifiers, research/crafting, and remote food/drink/feeding-machine routing. Receiver eligibility is checked on each transfer, and doses are limited to actual capacity.
- Turbo nutriment pump and mobility nanite core, using native internal-organ surgery, research, EMP/cooldown behavior, and trait cleanup. Original GS13 spawn paths are retained as aliases.
- Hydraulic movement assistance and calovoltaic MOD modules, using native installation, activation, power cores, research, and removal cleanup. Locked variants and the original module artwork are included. Powered hydraulic assistance supplies source-scoped immunity to the implemented helplessness threshold effects.
- Exoskeleton MODsuit bundle with native construction, a high-capacity cell, research, fixed-module variant, original sprites, and visible/invisible skins. Open frames retain body and modular-uniform panels while providing minimal armor and no environmental seal.
- Gain touch spell and its teaching tome, plus both Wand of Gluttony variants. They use native spell actions, learning, cooldowns, antimagic, gun charges, projectiles, and food chemistry, with source object/action sprites.
- Fattening, belch, and mixed trap conjuration spells, both teaching tomes, and original trap artwork. Native turf signals, caster-mind immunity, antimagic, charges, and expiration govern the traps.
- The source's five additional mining rewards are integrated into native necropolis tendril chests: calorite hammer, calorite whip, sprinkled Memento Calori, mixed grenade, and Gain tome. Each has the same chance as one native reward entry.
- Calorite glass alloy, shards, directional/full windows, and optical reflectors. Native smelting/protolathe designs, grinding, stack construction, air sealing, dismantling, and shard recycling preserve material quantities. Laser amplification follows actual crossings or successful reflections.
- Autolathe calorite storage and recovery, initial calorite-glass alloy recipes, native design-disk imports, and material-aware UI/queue checks. Upgraded machines preserve material value when printed items are recycled; stack batches merge at their full size.
- Adipolipus/lipofruit and munchies weed, using native planting, potency genetics, harvesting, seed extraction, grinding, and fermentation. Munchies weed mutates from native cannabis and retains orange bioluminescence. Its appetite stimulant respects the master and chemical preferences.
- Focused game regression tests covering these mechanics and their native integration; current counts and workflow evidence are recorded below.
- Resource filename case corrections in 22 existing Paradise files, required for the pinned upstream revision to compile on Linux.

## Still incomplete

Most of GS13 remains to be ported. Its source has 302 DM files in `modular_gs`, plus base-code modifications and inherited Bubber/Skyrat systems. Remaining areas include the rest of multistage body/clothing rendering (other species, body shapes, and clothing families), taur/body-shape anatomy, remaining helplessness mechanics, blueberry transformations and bursting, gases/MILLA integration, calorite material transmutation and mood/quirk effects, remaining power machinery, remaining weapons and spells, diseases and genetics, species and organs, quirks, maps, jobs, remaining research, food/drinks, and inherited systems. Digestive speech effects and most additional chemicals are still pending. The feeding-tube expansion and hacked palette currently omit blueberry juice and Starshine Neutron, whose transformation systems are still pending. Additional weapon families and cyborg tools remain pending. Collar recoloring and general vendor distribution remain pending; the three functional collar designs are available through research.

Rendering uses Paradise's appearance system. The first adapter covers ordinary human belly/hip stages and the grey modular-uniform family; other species, breast/body-shape customization, digitigrade/taur fits, and most clothing remain unadapted. Physical mass and BMI now use source body-size formulas. Temporary growth does not change measured mass; taur mass adjustments await taur anatomy. Chemistry uses Paradise's discrete reactions and temperature limits; TG pH/purity simulation is not implemented. Starting-weight and physical body-size edits apply on the next spawn; growth toggles apply during the round. During-round preference edits affect a body only when editing its original character slot; snapshots stay with disconnected bodies.

Source arithmetic fixes: changes report the actual bounded amount applied, muscle gain uses its configured multiplier, repeated withdrawal cannot stack the galbanic modifier without limit, and excess food cannot create an automatic negative fullness adjustment. Metabolism rays respect weapon preferences and complete armor immunity. Projectile weight effects use Paradise's armor equation. Door trapping listens on the turf instead of the door contents, only affects the entering mob, and retains its delay across intermediate body stages. The weight stun has an explicit tick interval and cannot overwrite a normal stun. Portable blending retains food after a partial transfer, and feeding reactions only apply to the dose that fits in the recipient. Adipoelectric generators account for actual consumed regular weight in joules before converting to watts; transformers consume their complete input rather than losing the first 500 kW from accounting. EMP state starts inactive and deletion always releases an occupant. Gun power costs are scaled to Paradise's 1,000-unit gun cell, preserving the source's ratios to its 10,000-unit cell.

## Testing this checkpoint

Use BYOND **516.1666**, Paradise's pinned stable version. Compile `paradise.dme` in Dream Maker and host it with Dream Daemon. Follow Paradise's existing setup instructions for the server and TGUI assets.

For an existing schema-73 database, apply `SQL/updates/73-74.sql` and update the configured SQL version to 74. A new database uses `SQL/paradise_schema.sql`. Without a database, preference edits are session-only.

Open **Character Setup → GS13: Weight preferences**, enable weight gain and the sources to test, save, and spawn. The autolathe's Medical category contains the weight analyzer; cargo offers the Body Composition Equipment Crate. Admin-spawnable items include `/obj/item/portable_weight_scanner`, `/obj/structure/scale`, `/obj/item/bluespace_belt`, and `/obj/item/reagent_containers/glass/bottle/lipoifier`.

Mine calorite and process it in the ORM, then load the sheets into the protolathe. Basic Fatoray research requires Combat 4, Materials 4, and Biotech 4; the cannon requires Combat 5. AL-T-Ray models require Combat 4, Materials 5, and Biotech 5. For direct testing, spawn `/obj/item/gun/energy/fatoray`, `/obj/item/gun/energy/gun/fattening`, or `/obj/item/gun/energy/laser/alter_ray/gainrate`. Grind calorite sheets for lipoifier and micro calorite. Enable **Allow severe weight effects** to test exposure accumulation and its persistent effects. Security cargo has three-gun Salamander and Matador crates. The uplink has an **Adipo-Warfare** category. Research unlocks the Basic Fatoray cyborg upgrade in the exosuit fabricator; install it after the cyborg selects a module. For melee testing, spawn `/obj/item/melee/chainofcommand/fattening` or `/obj/item/gavelhammer/fattening`; their `/permanent` subtypes use galbanic damage. Use calorite sheets in hand to craft floor tiles, a statue, or a door; apply sheets to normal/displaced girders for walls/falsewalls. The stronger, disguised, and dance floor variants are available for mapping/admin testing, matching the source's recipe availability. Native weight batons are `/obj/item/melee/baton/loaded/fattening` (and `/stunsword`) and `/obj/item/melee/classic_baton/fattening`.

For the new consumables, use the Adipo-Warfare uplink or spawn `/obj/item/grenade/chem_grenade/lipoifier`, `/obj/item/grenade/chem_grenade/galbanic`, `/obj/item/grenade/chem_grenade/fatmix`, `/obj/item/pen/calorite/syndicate`, and `/obj/item/gun/energy/kinetic_accelerator/crossbow/feeder`. Native gas masks block these smoke payloads. Pens require item effects; the crossbow requires weapon effects for injection and chemical effects for the resulting weight gain.

The Memento Calori pendant is available from the uplink or as `/obj/item/clothing/neck/necklace/memento_mori/calori`. Equip it in the neck slot and use its action to bind for four seconds. Removing it reveals concealed weight and adds 600 ordinary and 600 permanent weight, subject to the magic/permanent switches and configured cap. The same body cannot bind to that pendant again.

Cyborg feeding-tube research requires Biotech 2 and Programming 2; the expansion requires Biotech 4, Programming 3, and Materials 3. Install a base module before adding its expansion. Like the GS13 source, these tubes use hypospray injection mechanics; they are separate from the freestanding oral feeding machine. The base palette has cream, milk, and nutriment; expansion adds alien honey, strawberry milk, chocolate milk, and weak lipoifier. The hacked module also provides lipoifier, galbanic compound, and micro calorite. Native injection protection and chemical/permanent preferences still apply.

The Feeding Tube Crate is available in organic cargo. For direct testing, spawn `/obj/machinery/iv_drip/feeding_tube` or `/obj/item/portable_blender`. Drag the machine onto an adjacent recipient with an accessible mouth; apply held food to its blender, then use the control panel to adjust flow from 0 to 5 units per second, pause, disconnect, or eject. Alt-click toggles flow. Recipients can click their tube alert to disconnect while able to use their hands. Activate an ejected blender in hand to remove its beaker. Standalone open beakers can also be installed.

Adipoelectric circuit boards require Power Storage 3, Engineering 3, and Biotech 3. Build them with a native machine frame and the listed stock parts. Secure the platform over a cable junction, stand on it, and click to enter; move or resist to leave. Item effects govern gain/loss. Base generators consume up to five ordinary weight units per second and produce up to 250 kW. Transformers consume surplus power, with slower conversion above 500 kW. EMPs eject generator occupants and disable generation for sixty seconds; occupied transformers lock and convert at five times their normal rate for sixty seconds. Direct spawn paths are `/obj/machinery/power/adipoelectric_generator` and `/obj/machinery/power/adipoelectric_transformer`.

The Caloray protolathe design requires Power Storage 3, Materials 4, and Biotech 4, and produces a gun without a cell. Wrench the compartment open, insert a held power cell, and close it. Activate a closed gun to switch fatten/thin modes; activate an open gun to eject its cell. Fire at a carbon target with item effects enabled. Direct test variants are `/obj/item/gun/medbeam/caloray/charged`, `/empty_cell`, `/infinite_cell`, and `/bluespace_cell`. At the default gain/loss multipliers, a full fattening tick consumes 500 cell charge to add ten ordinary weight, while thinning removes up to 25 and returns 625 charge. Zero or inverted weight loss cannot generate charge. The source mech subtype is retained, but no new mech equipment module is distributed.

For appearance testing, spawn as a human with **Show weight body sprites** enabled in GS13 preferences. Visible stages follow apparent weight, so concealment equipment changes the silhouette. A normal grey jumpsuit adapts to the body and fullness stages; Ctrl-Shift-click rolls it down, and native dyeing recolors its panels. Departmental variants use `/obj/item/clothing/under/color/grey/medical`, `/service`, `/cargo`, `/engi`, `/science`, `/security`, `/security/blue`, and `/command`. These variants are currently directly spawnable; job outfits and wardrobe distribution are still pending. Native oversuits that hide jumpsuits also hide their expansion panels. Non-modular clothing retains its native shape.

Calorite collars require Materials 3 and Biotech 3 at the protolathe. They add 0.5 to the configured gain multiplier while worn, subject to item effects. Bluespace receiver/transmitter research requires Materials 4, Biotech 4, and Bluespace 3. Apply one collar to the other while holding it to link; wear the transmitter and place the receiver on a wearer with weight gain and item effects enabled. Food, drinks, and a connected freestanding feeding machine then deliver their contents to that receiver. Removing or disabling the receiver restores ordinary eating. A full receiver retains the undelivered supply. Alt-click the transmitter to unlink. Drag any collar onto yourself to open its small pocket; locking collars contain a key. Apply a held key to toggle the lock. Direct paths are `/obj/item/clothing/neck/human_petcollar/calorite`, `/locked/calorite`, `/locked/bluespace_collar_receiver`, and `/locked/bluespace_collar_transmitter`. Calorite crafting consumes a corresponding base collar plus three sheets, with a wrench and welder. Base and locking-only collars are currently available for admin testing; ordinary vendor distribution is pending.

The turbo nutriment pump requires Materials 5, Power Storage 4, and Biotech 4; the mobility nanite core requires Materials 4, Programming 4, and Biotech 4. Both are available from the protolathe and exosuit fabricator, then installed through native chest surgery. Their paths are `/obj/item/organ/internal/cyberimp/chest/nutriment/turbo` and `/obj/item/organ/internal/cyberimp/chest/mobility`. The pump adds fifty nutrition at or below the full threshold, at most once every five seconds, and retains native death/EMP checks. Food preferences govern weight gain from that nutrition. The core removes body-fat slowdown immediately while implanted; it does not remove other movement penalties or change weight.

The focused test build uses `GAME_TESTS`, `GS13_TESTS`, `CIBUILDING`, and `TEST_CONFIG_OVERRIDE="gs13_tests"`. Set `data/next_map.txt` to `/datum/map/test_tiny`, ensure `tmp/` exists, and run `tools/ci/run_server.sh`. The GS13 Actions workflow performs these steps when Actions is enabled for the fork. This focused suite disables SQL; it does not validate the database migration against a running database.

Hydraulic Assistance research requires Engineering 3 and Materials 3. Calovoltaic Generator research requires Engineering 3, Power Storage 3, and Biotech 3. Both use the native exosuit fabricator's MODsuit Modules category. Install them in a MOD controller, equip and activate the suit, then select the calovoltaic module through the native suit panel. Hydraulics passively reduce body-fat slowdown by two, at one native charge per two-second tick. Calovoltaic generation requests ten ordinary weight loss per second; the default loss multiplier burns five per second and returns 2.5 native charge per weight unit. Master opt-out and weight-loss immunity apply; permanent weight is preserved. A full, missing, or infinite core does not consume weight. Direct spawn paths are `/obj/item/mod/module/hydraulic_movement` and `/obj/item/mod/module/calovoltaic`, each with a `/locked` subtype. The complete `/obj/item/mod/control/pre_equipped/exoskeleton` is also researchable at those combined levels. Its `/locked` subtype fixes all three modules in place; both prefabs retain removable cells inside a fixed core. The `/obj/item/mod/construction/plating/exoskeleton` can finish an ordinary MOD assembly. It is an open support frame, not an EVA suit. The source invisible skin is available through the native skin-setting proc for admin testing. Remaining helplessness effects are still pending.

For magic testing, spawn `/obj/item/book/granter/spell/gain` (the source `/obj/item/book/granter/action/spell/gain` alias also works), read it, then activate **Gain** and touch an adjacent carbon target. It requests 100 ordinary weight through the magic preference, normally adding 50, and starts a thirty-second cooldown. Antimagic consumes the charged hand with the native half-cooldown refund. Upgrades retain ten-second reductions down to a quarter-second floor. A spent book turns to dust and applies the same magic-gated weight request to a subsequent reader. `/obj/item/gun/magic/wand/food` and `/overpowered` create native foods containing five or twenty units of lipoifier. Native wand randomization gives up to fifteen or one hundred charges; they do not regenerate. The food's later chemical effects require chemical preferences independently of magic. Gain also appears in native necropolis tendril chests. Trap tomes and food wands currently use direct/admin spawning, matching their lack of general distribution in the pinned source. The source xenoarchaeology research reward still needs a native counterpart.

Trap tomes use `/obj/item/book/granter/spell/gs13_traps` and `/belch`; source `/obj/item/book/granter/action/spell/traps_fattening` and `/traps_belch` aliases also work. They teach native `/datum/spell/aoe/conjure/gs13_traps/fattening` and `/belch`; the base spell mixes both types for admin testing. Each cast creates up to five single-use traps on distinct open tiles within three tiles, lasting five minutes, with a twenty-five-second initial cooldown. Caster immunity follows the mind across body transfers. Fattening traps request 150 magic weight, normally adding 75. Belch traps knock victims down for eight seconds; their burp and fullness relief use the chemical preference, while ordinary knockdown remains independent of weight preferences. Native antimagic blocks either effect while consuming the charge. Directly spawned `/obj/structure/trap/fattening` and `/belch` have unlimited charges with a one-minute recharge. Examining a nearby trap reveals it and consumes a charge, following the native trap behavior.

Calorite glass is an initial alloy in the autolathe and ore redemption machine, and a protolathe design. The autolathe accepts calorite alongside metal and glass; its Construction category prints either calorite sheets or the alloy. Compatible researched calorite designs can be imported on ordinary design disks. Other unsupported minerals and locked designs remain excluded. One sheet consumes 2,000 calorite and 1,000 glass material units. Use its stack menu for a directional window (one sheet), full window (two), or shard (one). Fasten windows with a screwdriver; unfasten and wrench them to recover their sheets. Breaking them produces calorite shards that weld back into the alloy. Grinding a sheet yields twenty silicon and two lipoifier. Finish a native reflector frame with five calorite-glass sheets, then weld it to the floor. Successful reflections and actual laser crossings add the larger of 25 weight energy or 10%, capped at 5,000 above the projectile's initial payload. Directional panes apply this only across their glazed edge; full panes apply it once on entry. Collision probes, bullets, failed reflections, and teleportation across distant tiles do not add energy. Weapon preferences still govern the resulting beam hit. Direct paths are `/obj/item/stack/sheet/calorite_glass`, `/obj/item/shard/calorite_glass`, `/obj/structure/window/calorite`, `/fulltile`, and `/obj/structure/reflector/single/calorite`.

Adipolipus seeds use `/obj/item/seeds/lipoplant` and produce `/obj/item/food/grown/lipofruit`; they currently require direct/admin spawning because their source vendor and xenoarchaeology reward are not ported. One harvest consumes the plant. Munchies weed is available through native cannabis mutations or `/obj/item/seeds/cannabis/munchies`, and supports repeated harvesting. At their default potency, lipofruit contains four lipoifier; munchies leaves contain two appetite stimulant and one space drugs. Potency changes propagate through harvested produce and extracted seeds. Native grinding extracts these chemicals, and fermentation retains them while producing coconut liqueur or fruit wine. Appetite stimulant reduces fullness by fifteen per normal life cycle after recovery, without removing nutrition. Ordinary narcotic effects remain governed by Paradise chemistry. Milkmelons are available through native watermelon mutations, produce milk, slice into five portions while conserving their chemical contents, and juice into milk. Blueberry transformations remain pending.

## Resume notes

The published work lives on `bobatochaps/Paradisefunnything:gs13-paradise-port`; the fork's master branch is unchanged. The source and target pins above are intentional. Resume from this branch rather than reconstructing changes from an older checkout. Port code is concentrated in `code/modules/gs13/`, with native integration changes in the corresponding Paradise systems. Regression tests are in `code/tests/test_gs13_weight.dm` and `code/tests/test_gs13_body_size.dm`; `.github/workflows/gs13-port.yml` builds and runs them on each branch push.

The freestanding machine and portable blender are now implemented in `feeding_machine.dm` and `portable_blender.dm`. The six added integration tests cover partial blending, container swaps, timed flow, capacity/refilling, source preferences, and connection cleanup. These tests pass in the native BYOND run linked above. Fixtures must explicitly place the operator, target, and machine together because `allocate()` otherwise picks random room tiles. Source `adipoelectric_generator.dm` and `adipoelectric_transformer.dm` are adapted in `code/modules/gs13/adipoelectric.dm`; their five native integration tests pass. They cover wattage versus fuel consumption, shared surplus accounting, EMP expiry, reference cleanup, real machine-frame construction, RPED upgrades, and recovered components. Paradise uses `produce_direct_power`, `consume_direct_power`, and `get_surplus` on regional powernets; these values are watts, so do not multiply generated power by the processing interval a second time. Transformer consumption must include its first 500 kW, which the source drops above the nonlinear threshold. A zero EMP timer must not activate startup overdrive or trap the occupant. The Caloray is now adapted in `code/modules/gs13/caloray.dm`; its six native integration tests pass in the linked run. Native `cell.use()` returns a boolean, whereas the source returns charge consumed. The thin mode must multiply actual regular weight removed by 25, limit requests to free capacity, and never charge for disabled/zero/inverted loss. The medbeam crossing check is factored into an overridable hook; ordinary medical beams retain their explosion behavior.
